# Project card illustrations

These default covers are original line-art illustrations drawn to match the
checkmarkaudio.com “selected art” and Team page clip-art style:

- Gold monochrome line work on a dark field (site gold is `#B8904F`; here the
  stroke is themed with the active portal `--color-gold` token via CSS mask)
- Even stroke, rounded caps and joins
- Occasional flat fills (piano keys, fader caps, lamp shade)
- Studio subjects: microphone, headphones, piano, vinyl/turntable, guitar and
  amp, mixing desk (VU rack + faders), live mic and waveform, studio monitors,
  a late-night lamp and record

Source references from Bridget’s site repo (`/workspace/reel/mock`):

- `MEDIA/IMAGES/BY_PAGE/team/clipart/` — `logo-headphones.png`, `grand-piano.png`, `rack-unit-vu-meters.png`
- `MEDIA/ARTWORK/` — `live-recording-microphone-stand-gold.png`, `live-recording-guitar-amplifier.png`, `mastering-compact-disc-gold.png`, mixing fader rack, patchbay cable
- Team page inline SVGs for the waveform and fader bank (`team.html`)
- Brand icon: gold headphones-and-mic (`MEDIA/IMAGES/icons/checkmark-audio-icon-192.png`)
